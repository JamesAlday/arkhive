The Grand Council is the governing body of the Well, composed of the heads of the Major Arcana guilds. Each member represents a primary function of the city and serves as a direct conduit for Xod's will. The Council deliberates on matters of policy, law, and spiritual guidance, ensuring that the Great Cycle is maintained and that the Well operates in harmony with its divine purpose.

The Grand Council was deposed after the Heist at the Chancellor's Mansion during the Great Reset, leading to a period of uncertainty and reorganization within the Well's governance.
