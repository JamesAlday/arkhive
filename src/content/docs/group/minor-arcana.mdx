The Minor Arcana consists of guilds that perform essential labor and maintenance within the Well, yet are deliberately excluded from the Grand Council. Though their work keeps the city functioning, their social and spiritual status is deliberately lower. This distinction reminds all citizens that importance in labor does not equal authority in governance or proximity to Xod's will.

## 13. The Forgers
![Forgers](https://i.imgur.com/dEVk12g.jpeg)

Responsible for manufacturing, metalworking, and fabrication of tools, structural components, and machinery. They ensure the Axis and Luminary have the materials needed to keep the Well standing and powered.

## 14. The Runners
![Runners](https://i.imgur.com/6mhzOjE.jpeg)

Couriers, porters, and internal logistics specialists. They ferry messages, materials, and occasionally people between levels and guilds, acting as the veins through which the Well pulses.

## 15. The Breathers
![Breathers](https://i.imgur.com/gDcTwJZ.jpeg)

Regulators of air, climate, and pressure. They monitor ventilation, purification, and environmental control, keeping each level within acceptable parameters of health and habitability.

## 16. The Menders
![Menders](https://i.imgur.com/c3461nO.jpeg)

Medical, biological, and emergency care. They manage both routine health and outbreak responses, including quarantine measures, healing magic, and experimental therapies.

## 17. The Kindlers
![Kindlers](https://i.imgur.com/38IDNQA.jpeg)

Providers and managers of energy, fuel, and lighting beyond the Luminary's scope. They ensure the cycle of illumination and warmth continues without interruption.

## 18. The Shapers
![Shapers](https://i.imgur.com/XXBf4no.jpeg)

Interior construction and habitat management. They build and maintain living quarters, workplaces, and public spaces, ensuring that the physical environment supports both labor and order.
\column

## 19. The Tenders
![Tenders](https://i.imgur.com/ArpGijc.jpeg)

Agricultural guilds that manage demiplane farms, gardens, and animal husbandry. Though they are the source of most food and raw materials, their spiritual rank remains modest despite their importance.


## 20. The Scriveners
![Scriveners](https://i.imgur.com/Xc6SRc3.jpeg)

Educators, trainers, and recorders of minor lore. They instruct apprentices, maintain manuals, and ensure the skill of the next generation aligns with the Great Cycle.

## 21. The Reckoners
![Scriveners](https://i.imgur.com/QCZhspM.png)

Accountants, statisticians, and metric-keepers. They track production, labor quotas, and resource allocation, ensuring efficiency remains measurable and deviations are noted.

## 22. The Keepers
![Tenders](https://i.imgur.com/XVss1PK.jpeg)


Maintenance crews for machinery, stairs, portals, and structural systems. Though often unseen, they prevent collapse and disruption, keeping the Well in constant motion.

## The Unbound
The guilds represent those workers formally recognized by the state, but there are plenty of folks who slip between the cracks. Those without a guild are known as "unbound" and live outside the light of the Great Cycle.
