The Major Arcana consists of twelve guilds charged with the most critical functions of the Well. Each guild is considered a primary expression of the Cycle, and each head serves on the **Grand Council**, the deliberative body that guides policy and enforces harmony throughout the Well. Together, they ensure the city operates with mechanical precision and spiritual conformity.

## 1. The Supreme Chancellor
![Chancellor](https://i.imgur.com/qg1zV3u.jpeg)

The living embodiment of Xod's will. While he may not directly manage the operations of the Well, his presence sanctifies the Cycle. He intervenes only when divine interpretation or extraordinary circumstances requires it, and his word is law.

## 2. The Ascendant
![Ascendant](https://i.imgur.com/ZXQEMDE.png)

The Ascendant comprises the clergy and spiritual authority for the Chancellor and Xod's worship within the Well. They are responsible for the performance of various rituals, consecrating currency, and maintaining the moral alignment of both citizens and guilds. Their interpretation of the Great Cycle underpins all law and custom.

## 3. The Veiled Hand
![Concordant](https://i.imgur.com/5ulFLHE.jpeg)

The Veiled Hand is the Chancellor's security and surveillance guild. Their eyes watch for treason, heresy, and disorder. They answer only to the Chancellor, and even the Arbiters rarely have authority over them.

## 4. The Concordant
![Concordant](https://i.imgur.com/elGx5qe.jpeg)

The Concordant act as coordinators and mediators for the other guilds. They help to keep systems synchronized and ensure harmony flows from kept promises. They mediate disputes between guilds, set exchange values, and approve new guild charters.

## 5. The Custodian
![Custodian](https://i.imgur.com/vqCwXE0.jpeg)

Portal maintenance and dimensional interfaces. This guild controls the access, distribution, maintenance, and supply of dimensional doorways.
::

## 6. The Herald
![Herald](https://i.imgur.com/C9VG666.jpeg)

To name a thing is to make it real. The Herald covers all aspects of communications and records keeping. The Well's history is officially recorded by this guild, as well as the maintenance of public records, and certifications or licenses are registered with them.

## 7. The Provider
![Provider](https://i.imgur.com/2ZOkhP3.jpeg)

The Providers deal with food and resource distribution. They control the agricultural planning for demiplane farms, set rations, and determine distribution of food and other goods.

## 8. The Purifier
![Purifier](https://i.imgur.com/asDcXNe.jpeg)

The Purifier deals with both the recycling and purifying of resources, from water to waste, and even disease control. They also deal with recycling, filtration, and biological hazards. The guild heads are responsible for enforcing purity laws and help to define what is unclean.

## 9. The Shepherd
![Shepherd](https://i.imgur.com/D8xWNqg.jpeg)

The Shepherds help citizens in the Well find their proper placement within the Great Cycle. Like the farmer tending to his flock, Shepherds help citizens to find Xod's will to take the right path. They manage workforce optimization to make sure each soul finds its appointed task. They also regulate reproduction and marriage licences, and approve requests for migration between levels.

## 10. The Arbiter
![Arbiter](https://i.imgur.com/JfWq2cC.jpeg)

The Arbiter are the scales upon which justice is weighed. They act to keep the Well in balance, not out of fairness, but out of necessity. The Arbiters interpret Xod's will and inflict punishment upon those who deviate.
:

## 11. The Axis
![Axis](https://i.imgur.com/MoiSZJN.jpeg)

The Axis comprises the various engineers, scientists, and craftsmen who work to keep The Well in one piece. Configure load distribution and ensure vertical stability. The Cycle stands because The Axis holds it.

## 12. The Luminary
![Luminary](https://i.imgur.com/6vTWeBa.jpeg)

The Luminary are responsible for light and power regulation throughout the Well. They maintain various lighting and secondary lighting sources and help to maintain and control power and lighting levels throughout the Well.
