[Ozwald Firemountain](/npc/ozwald-firemountain) is the Custodian of the Well who told [Lilac](/pc/lilac) about a portal that has been undergoing maintenance for a long time.

It contains a caravan that has been going for years without end. The records show each person who was sent to examine encountered a moving caravan that never reached its destination.
