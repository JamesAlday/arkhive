This demiplane was originally created as an orchard for fruiting trees (apples, peaches, cherries, etc). Over time, the orchard has grown to include a forest and part of a mountain. 

The party was called in to investigate reports of farmers being attacked by rats of unusual size. The party tracked the rat marks back to a cave in the mountain guarded by wererats. 

The party killed the wererats and rescued the children that had been taken by the rats.

Farmers who had been cursed by the wererats were kept in quarantine but later found murdered.
