All things in the Well are measured, even its citizens. From the first recorded breath they take, to the day of placement within a guild, each citizen is observed, evaluated, and aligned with the Great Cycle. Level of birth, rings of status, education, apprenticeship, and labor - all are threads woven into a life designed to serve the turning of the whole. In the Well, identity is not discovered in wilderness or won in distant kingdoms. It is assigned, refined, and synchronized.

## Origin Level
The foremost feature that shapes every citizen of the Well is the level of their birth. It determines not only your access to light, space, and resources, but also determines your expectations, education, and place within the Great Cycle. Whether you were raised among the bright, quiet corridors of the Uppers, the structured industry of the Mids, or the crowded labor of the Lowers, your origin leaves an indelible mark on how you speak, how you work, and how others judge you. Though travel between levels is possible, true movement is rare. Your original level shapes how others see you, what you take for granted, and how close, or distant, you have always been from the Sun.

Your Original Level does not change your ability scores or class progression. It does, however, shape your starting resources, social footing, and the way the Well responds to you.

#### The Uppers
Raised among administrative halls, temples, and well-lit districts, Uppers begin with relative comfort.

- **Starting Funds**: 50 gp
- **Currency Access**: Gold, Silver
- **Clothing**: Fine or well-tailored attire
- **Social Assumption**: Competent, educated, trustworthy
- **Origin Feature**: **Privileged Bearing**: In Upper and Mid districts, you can secure modest lodging or an audience with minor officials without cost. In Lower districts, you are conspicuous.

#### Mids (Upper)
Clerks, junior administrators, guild coordinators, and white-collar functionaries who stand just below the Uppers.

- **Starting Funds**: 30 gp
- **Currency Access**: Primarily Gold and Silver
- **Clothing**: Professional attire or guild-marked formal wear
- **Social Assumption**: Competent, connected, respectable
- **Origin Features**: **Bureaucratic Fluency**: You understand how to navigate paperwork, requisitions, and administrative channels. You can reduce the time required to secure permits, licenses, or minor official approvals in Upper and Mid districts.

#### Mids (Lower)
The backbone of the Well. Skilled laborers, technicians, and guild workers who keep systems functioning day-to-day.

- **Starting Funds**: 20 gp
- **Currency Access**: Primarily Silver, some Gold and Copper
- **Clothing**: Durable but well-maintained work attire
- **Social Assumption**: Reliable, industrious
- **Origin Features**: **Guild Familiarity**: You know which guild controls a given space or function and how to approach them properly for basic assistance.

#### Lowers
Raised among heavy industry, recycling systems, and reclamation sectors, the Lowers survive by resilience.

- **Starting Funds**: 100 cp
- **Currency Access**: Copper
- **Clothing**: Worn but serviceable work clothes
- **Social Assumption**: Rough, capable, suspect
- **Origin Features**: **Hard-Learned Resilience**: When making an ability check related to enduring physical hardship (heat, cold, hunger, exhaustion, etc) you may reroll once per long rest and take the new result.


## Species
The Well was not always as it is now. In the earliest Cycles, its population consisted almost entirely of what are now called Baseline Humanoids - humans, elves, dwarves, halflings, and other standard lineages recognized in the common records. They are still present, still respected, and still form much of the administrative and guild leadership of the Well. Yet over time, subtle changes have emerged within the population.

No official proclamation acknowledges it, but the truth is widely understood: the Well changes those who live within it.

#### Baseline Humanoids
Baseline Humanoids represent the closest living descendants of the Well's original inhabitants. They show no visible mutation or deviation from their ancestral forms. Within official doctrine, they are held up as examples of "stable alignment" with the Great Cycle - proof that the system functions as intended.

In practice, Baseline citizens are often perceived as predictable, adaptable, and socially stable. They are common in the Uppers and within the Major Arcana, though they can be found at every level. While they may not display unusual traits, their strength lies in cultural continuity and institutional familiarity. They are often trusted with roles requiring mediation, diplomacy, or long-term oversight.

Though they are no longer the majority, they are still the standard against which deviation is measured.

#### Mutated Humanoids
Over many Cycles, exposure to unknown environmental factors within the Well - arcane resonance, demiplane bleed-through, generational adaptation, or something older and unrecorded, has produced subtle and not-so-subtle mutations among the populace.

These modified/mutated humanoids share ancestry with Baseline species but exhibit additional physical or mental alterations. Some possess unusual sensory capabilities, hardened skin, bioluminescent markings, altered metabolisms, psionic talents, or other traits beyond their lineage's norm.

The state does not deny their existence, but it does not formally classify them. Mutation is treated as a personal characteristic, not a social category. To discuss it structurally is considered impolite, and even dangerous. The Ascendant teaches that all variation is merely another expression of Xod's infinite design - yet the Veiled Hand keeps careful records.

Most citizens live alongside mutated humanoids without incident. In some guilds, particularly the Breathers, Kindlers, and Custodians, mutations are quietly valued. In others, especially in the Uppers, they are regarded with unease.

No one agrees on what caused the change. Officially, nothing has changed at all.

#### Awakened Animals
More rarely, certain animals within the Well, or within its demiplanes, have manifested intelligence, speech, and humanoid posture. These Awakened beings include lineages such as kenku, aarakocra, lizardfolk, and other recognized species.

Their origins are debated. Some claim they are the result of arcane experiments in forgotten Cycles, while others whisper that the demiplanes themselves reshape life that lingers in them for too long. The Ascendant maintains that they too are expressions of Xod's boundless design.

Awakened citizens are uncommon but not unknown. Many serve in guilds where their natural aptitudes are advantages. Aarakocra may work with the Breathers in high shafts. Lizardfolk often labor among the Tenders in humid demiplanes. Kenku frequently appear among the Heralds and Runners.

They are not considered monsters. They are citizens, though always somewhat apart.

#### Awakened Flora
Rarest of all citizens are the Awakened Plants - ambulatory beings grown rather than born, capable of thought and speech. Their forms vary widely, from bare-skinned humanoids, vine-limbed figures, fungal symbiotes, or flower-crowned wanderers.

Some claim they emerged from agricultural demiplanes. Others believe they are the Well correcting some unseen imbalance. Their biology is often difficult to classify, and the Menders maintain special protocols for their care.

Theologically, they pose quite the dilemma. If the Cycle governs all life, what does it mean when life rearranges itself?

Plant-folk are uncommon enough to draw attention, but not so rare as to be myth. Most serve in guilds connected to growth, reclamation, or environmental regulation. Their presence is tolerated - sometimes celebrated - but never fully explained.
