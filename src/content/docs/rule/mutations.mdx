---
title: Mutations
description: Various mutations found in The Well
---

Mutations are not officially recognized within the Well, though no one can claim ignorance about them. Most of them are small alterations - odd talents, unusual features, or subtle differences a person learns to live with, and some even appear beneficial in certain lines of work. 

Others are kept private, either out of caution or social pressure. In rarer cases, the changes are more dramatic, drawing unwanted attention or discomfort from those around them. The Ascendant teaches that all things occur within the Great Cycle, though few authorities are eager to discuss what these changes might mean.
