---
title: Household Devotion
description: Household gods and unofficial religions in The Well
---

Though Xod is the One and the Great Cycle is indivisible, the people of the Well have always sought names and faces for the forces that shape their daily lives. Over generations, certain aspects of the Cycle have been personified as **Household Gods** - localized, approachable reflections of the greater design.

The Ascendant teaches that these are not separate deities but minor expressions of Xod's infinite structure. Just as a gear may be named without ceasing to be part of the machine, so too may an aspect of the Cycle be invoked without dividing the One.

Shrines to Household Gods are common in private dwellings, workshops, guild halls, and demiplane outposts. They preside over hearths, stairwells, harvest rows, ledger books, and air ducts. Some are officially tolerated and even quietly encouraged. Others are watched more carefully by the Veiled Hand.

For clerics and other devoted characters, a Household God provides focus without heresy. Publicly, all devotion is directed toward Xod. Privately, prayers often carry another name.

#### Recognized Household Gods
Below are widely acknowledged aspects of Xod, grouped loosely by domain and function. None are considered independent beings in official doctrine, though common belief often tells a more complicated story.

##### Aurix, the First Spark
- **Aspect**: Light, Renewal, Beginnings
- **Domains**: Light, Knowledge, Renewal
- **Disposition**: Orderly, hopeful
- **Guild**: Luminary

Aurix represents illumination and the ignition of motion. Invoked at dawn-cycle shifts and during the Great Reset, Aurix is the patron of fresh starts, invention, and revelation. Shrines often feature small mirrored discs or ever-burning lamps.

##### Thren, the Unblinking
- **Aspect**: Vigilance, Hidden Truths
- **Domains**: Knowledge, Twilight, Trickery
- **Disposition**: Neutral, severe
- **Guild**: Veiled Hand

Thren sees what others miss. Patron of watchers, investigators, and those who work in shadow. Thren is invoked for clarity in deception and strength in secrecy. Officially framed as the "observational function" of Xod.

##### Kharos, the Even-Handed
- **Aspect**: Balance, Consequence
- **Domains**: Order, Justice, War
- **Disposition**: Lawful, impartial
- **Guild**: Arbiters

Kharos weighs action against outcome. Prayers to Kharos are offered before judgments, contracts, and punishments. Some believe Kharos is merciless, others say he is perfectly fair.

##### Myr Vale, Keeper of the Green Veins
- **Aspect**: Growth, Sustenance
- **Domains**: Nature, Life
- **Dispositions**: Patient, cyclical
- **Guild**: Tenders, Providers

Myr Vale governs crops, livestock, and living systems. Farmers in demiplanes commonly leave offerings of first fruits. Plant-folk often revere Myr Vale most strongly.

##### Veyra of the Open Path
- **Aspect**: Travel, Safe Passage
- **Domains**: Travel, Protection, Twilight
- **Dispositions**: Free-moving, observant
- **Guild**: Runners, Custodians

Veyra is invoked before long stair descents or portal crossings. Often represented by a simple looped cord or an open doorway symbol. Popular among adventurers in the Well.

##### Haldrin the Measure
- **Aspect**: Labor, Craft, Endurance
- **Domains**: Forge, Knowledge, Strength
- **Dispositions**: Stoic, reliable
- **Guild**: Forgers, Axis, Shapers

Haldrin blesses tools and steady hands. Those who work with metal, stone, or structure call upon him before beginning difficult tasks. His symbol is often a simple hammer over a vertical line.

##### Seris the Gentle Flame
- **Aspect**: Hearth, Family, Reconciliation
- **Domains**: Life, Peace
- **Dispositions**: Compassionate, restorative
- **Guild**: Shepherds

Seris presides over forgiveness, domestic harmony, and the quiet work of raising children within the Cycle. She is most invoked during the Day of Reset. 

##### Uloth the Boundless
- **Aspect**: Mutation, Adaptation
- **Domains**: Nature, Knowledge, Arcana
- **Dispositions**: Uncertain, transformative
- **Guild**: Breathers, Menders

Uloth is rarely spoken of openly, but is widely acknowledged in private. Said to represent Xod's "adaptive function", Uloth governs change, evolution, and deviation. Some Mutated Humans leave offerings in hidden shrines bearing abstract spiral patterns. 

Official doctrine calls Uloth "misinterpreted alignment." The Veiled Hand keeps records of his shrines.

##### Nemei of the Quiet Root
- **Aspect**: Stillness, Decay
- **Domains**: Grave, Nature
- **Dispositions**: Calm, inevitable
- **Guild**: Purifier

Nemei governs compost, recycling, endings, and the return of matter to function. Not a god of death, but of reclamation. Particularly revered by plant-folk and waste-workers.

##### Aethren the Unfettered
- **Aspect**: Inspiration, Unstructured Thought
- **Domains**: Trickery, Knowledge, Arcana
- **Dispositions**: Chaotic-leaning, inventive
- **Guild**: No guild association; tolerated but monitored

Artists, radicals, and certain Scriveners invoke Aethren in secret. Represents creativity, innovation, and ideas that strain against rigid structures.
