In the Well, who you are begins with who you serve. The Guilds are not merely professions - they are the living machinery of the Great Cycle. From the moment a citizen is assigned their appointed path, their guild shapes their training, their loyalties, and their place within the hierarchy of the levels.

#### 1. The Ascendant
Those who serve the Supreme Chancellor and the highest echelons of authority. The Ascendant believes stability is sacred and hierarchy is mercy.

- **Typical Roles**: Chancellor's Aide, Policy Architect, Ministerial Envoy, Internal Auditor
- **Ability Scores**: Int, Wis, Cha
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Insight, Religion
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Calligrapher's Supplies
- **Feat**: Magic Initiate (Cleric) or Skilled (Insight/Persuasion focus)
- **Starting Equipment**: Calligrapher's Supplies, Book (Great Cycle), Holy Symbol, Parchment (10 sheets), Robe, 8 GP
- **Feature**: **Voice of Authority**: You can request minor bureaucratic favors from civil functionaries and gain access to restricted administrative spaces.

#### 2. The Concordant
Diplomats and mediators who maintain harmony between guilds and levels. They prevent quiet tensions from becoming open fractures.

- **Typical Roles**: Mediator, Guild Liaison, Conflict Arbiter, Treaty Scribe
- **Ability Scores**: Str, Int, Cha
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Insight, Persuasion
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Gaming Set or Calligrapher's Supplies
- **Feat**: Alert or Skilled (Insight/Persuasion focus)
- **Starting Equipment**: Dagger, Calligrapher's Supplies, Fine Clothes, Ink, 5 Ink Pens, Parchment (9 sheets), 13 GP
- **Feature**: **Brokered Peace**: You can call for formal mediation to temporarily halt hostilities in social or civic disputes.

#### 3. The Custodian
Keepers of infrastructure and sacred mechanisms. The Well runs because Custodians never stop watching it.

- **Typical Roles**: System Engineer, Vault Warden, Shaft Inspector, Mechanism Steward
- **Ability Score**: Dex, Int, Wis
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Investigation, Perception
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Tinker's Tools
- **Feat**: Skilled (Investigation)
- **Starting Equipment**: Tinker's Tools, Fine Clothes, Lamp, Oil (3 flasks), Parchment (12 sheets), 23 GP
- **Feature**: **Infrastructure Access**: You know maintenance passages and can access controlled utility corridors.

#### 4. The Herald
Messengers, announcers, and rumor-weavers. Information is power, and the Heralds move it.

- **Typical Roles**: Public Announcer, Broadside Writer, Signal Operator, Cycle Historian
- **Ability Score**: Str, Dex, Int
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Persuasion, Investigation
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Musical Instrument or Artisan's Tools
- **Feat**: Alert or Choose 2 additional languages
- **Starting Equipment**: Artisan's Tools or Musical Instrument, 2 Pouches, Traveler's Clothes, 32 GP
- **Feature**: **Fast Passage**: You can requisition rapid travel through guild routes when bearing official dispatches.

#### 5. The Provider
Agriculturalists, quartermasters, and distributors of sustenance. They ensure no level starves.
- **Typical Roles**: Hydroponics Tender, Quartermaster, Ration Overseer, Brewer
- **Ability Score**: Str, Con, Wis
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Animal Handling, Nature
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Carpenter's Tools
- **Feat**: Crafter or Tough
- **Starting Equipment**: Sickle, Carpenter's Tools, Healer's Kit, Iron Pot, Shovel, Traveler's Clothes, 30 GP
- **Feature**: **Supply Chain**: You can secure modest provisions for yourself and companions in settled areas.

#### 6. The Purifier
Wardens of air, water, and waste. They work where others refuse.

- **Typical Roles**: Water Filtration Adept, Waste Reclaimer, Hazard Diver, Air Scrubber
- **Ability Scores**: Con, Int, Wis
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Nature, Survival
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Herbalism Kit
- **Feat**: Tavern Brawler or Skilled
- **Starting Equipment**: Shortbow, 20 Arrows, Herbalism Kit, Bedroll, Blanket, Pouch, Tent, Traveler's Clothes, 13 GP
- **Feature**: **Hazard Tolerance**: You and allies gain advantage on checks to navigate contaminated or foul environments (DM discretion).

#### 7. The Shepherd
The Shepherds guide citizens into their proper place within the Great Cycle, discerning Xod’s will in matters of labor, marriage, migration, and birth.

- **Typical Roles**: Workforce Assessor, Marriage Licensor, Reproductive Registrar, Migration Clerk, Vocational Evaluator
- **Abiliy Scores**: Con, Int, Wis
- **Skill Proficiencies**: History, Religion
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Calligrapher's Supplies
- **Feat**: Skilled (Insight focus) or Magic Initiate (Wizard)
- **Starting Equipment**: Quarterstaff, Calligrapher's Supplies, Book (Well history), Parchment (8 sheets), Robe, 8 GP
- **Feature**: **Appointed Path**: You can access civic records regarding employment, lineage, and level assignment

#### 8. The Arbiter
Judges and enforcers of Well law.

- **Typical Roles**: Magistrate, Tribunal Clerk, Peace Officer, Inquisitor
- **Ability Scores**: Str. Int, Wis
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Athletics, Intimidation
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Gaming Set
- **Feat**: Alert or Tough
- **Starting Equipment**: Spear, Light Crossbow, 20 Bolts, Gaming Set, Hooded Lantern, Manacles, Quiver, Traveler's Clothes, 12 GP
- **Feature**: **Legal Authority**: You can demand parley under Well law and compel civic cooperation in minor matters.

#### 9. The Axis
Maintainers of the lattice structure of the Well itself.

- **Typical Roles**: Stair Marshal, Shaft Technician, Traffic Regulator, Structural Engineer
- **Ability Scores**: Str, Dex, Wis
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Acrobatics, Perception
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Navigator's Tools
- **Feat**: Crafter or Lucky
- **Starting Equipment**: Dagger, Climber's Kit, Rope, Traveler's Clothes, 20 GP
- **Feature**: **Vertical Network**: You know optimal routes between levels and can reduce travel time in structured environments.

#### 10. The Luminary
Regulators of light and power throughout the Well.

- **Typical Roles**: Light Regulator, Power Grid Technician, Illumination Engineer, Reset Coordinator
- **Ability Scores**: Int, Wis, Cha
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Arcana, Investigation
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Jeweler's Tools
- **Feat**: Crafter or Magic Initiate (Wizard)
- **Starting Equipment**: Quarterstaff, Jeweler's Tools, Pouch, Robe, Shovel, Waterskin, 16 GP
- **Feature**: **Grid Access**: You understand the Well's lighting and secondary power systems. Given time and access, you can adjust illumination levels in a local area or identify disruptions in power flow. You are also granted limited access to controlled lighting systems in most districts.

#### 11. The Forgers
Smiths and industrial artisans shaping the Well's tools and arms.

- **Typical Roles**: Blacksmith, Machinist, Armorer, Structural Fabricator
- **Ability Scores**: Str, Int, Wis
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Athletics, Perception
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Smith's Tools
- **Feat**: Crafter or Tough
- **Starting Equipment**: Spear, Smith's Tools, Bullseye Lantern, Oil (5 Flasks), Tinderbox, Traveler's Clothes, 9 GP
- **Feature**: **Maker's Mark**: Your crafted goods carry guild credibility and fetch fair value.

#### 12. The Runners
Couriers and porters. They ensure people and things get to where they're supposed to be.

- **Typical Roles**: Courier, Porter, Escort, Runner, Scout 
- **Ability Scores**: Dex, Wis, Cha
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Athletics, Stealth
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Thieves' Tools
- **Feat**: Alert or Lucky
- **Starting Equipment**: Dagger, Thieves' Tools, Gaming Set, Bedroll, 2 Pouches, Traveler's Clothes, 10 GP
- **Feature**: **Informal Routes**: You know unofficial shortcuts and black-market contacts.

#### 13. The Breathers
Airflow engineers and atmospheric stewards.

- **Typical Roles**: Ventilation Tech, Pressure Monitor, Climate Surveyor
- **Ability Scores**: 
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Nature, Investigation
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Tinker's Tools
- **Feat**: Alert or Magic Initiate (Druid)
- **Starting Equipment**: Quarterstaff, Tinker's Tools, Bedroll, Bell, Pouch, Robe, String, Traveler's Clothes, Waterskin, 38 GP
- **Feature**: **Read the Air**: You can detect subtle environmental shifts quickly.


#### 14. The Menders
Medical practitioners and biological researchers.

- **Typical Roles**: Chirurgeon, Genetic Researcher, Apothecary, Field Medic
- **Ability Scores**: Str, Con, Wis
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Medicine, Investigation
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Herbalism Kit
- **Feat**: Healer or Skilled
- **Starting Equipment**: Quarterstaff, Healer's Kit, Bedroll, Book (Medicine), Lamp, Oil (3 Flasks), Traveler's Clothes, 16 GP
- **Feature**: **Clinical Authority**: You can requisition medical supplies in emergencies.

#### 15. The Kindlers
Energy technicians and lightkeepers

- **Typical Roles**: Reactor Attendant, Light Engineer, Power Distributor
- **Ability Scores**: Con, Int, Cha
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Arcana, Investigation
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Gaming Set
- **Feat**: Magic Initiate (Wizard) or Spellfire Spark
- **Starting Equipment**: Gaming Set, Arcane Focus (Crystal/Wand), 2 Pouches, Traveler's Clothes, 36 GP
- **Feature**: **Power Access**: You can temporarily reroute minor power sources with time and tools.

#### 16. The Shapers
Architects and structural designers of the Well's living spaces.

- **Typical Roles**: Architect, Structural Analyst, Expansion Planner
- **Ability Scores**: Dex, Con, Int
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Investigation, History
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Mason's Tools
- **Feat**: Crafter or Skilled
- **Starting Equipment**: Dagger, Light Hammer, Mason's Tools, Backpack, Bedroll, Crowbar, Ladder, Pole, 2 Pouches, Rope, String, Tinderbox, 5 Torches, Traveler's Clothes, Waterskin, 26 GP
- **Feature**: **Structural Insight**: You quickly assess structural weaknesses and safe modifications.

#### 17. The Tenders
Caretakers of flora, fungi, and bio-domes.

- **Typical Roles**: Botanist, Fungal Cultivator, Arboretum Keeper
- **Ability Scores**: Dex, Con, Wis
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Nature, Survival
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Herbalism Kit
- **Feat**: Magic Initiate (Druid) or Skilled
- **Starting Equipment**: Shortbow, 20 Arrows, Herbalism Kit, Bedroll, Blanket, Pouch, Tent, Traveler's Clothes, 13 GP
- **Feature**: **Green Network**: You can secure natural remedies and safe rest areas in cultivated spaces.

#### 18. The Scriveners
Record-keepers and archivists of the Great Cycle.

- **Typical Roles**: Census Clerk, Data Archivist, Ledger Keeper
- **Ability Scores**: Dex, Int, Wis
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Investigation, History
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Calligrapher's Supplies
- **Feat**: Lucky or Skilled
- **Starting Equipment**: Calligrapher's Supplies, Fine Clothes, Lamp, Oil (3 Flasks), Parchment (12 sheets), 23 GP
- **Feature**: **Paper Trail**: You can uncover or suppress minor bureaucratic records. 

#### 19. The Reckoners
Economists and auditors who track debts and credits.

- **Typical Roles**: Tax Assessor, Ledger Auditor, Market Analyst
- **Ability Scores**: Con, Int, Cha
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Insight, Investigation
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Forgery Kit
- **Feat**: Alert or Lucky
- **Starting Equipment**: Forgery Kit, Fine Clothes, Ink, Parchment (10 sheets), 15 GP
- **Feature**: **Debt Leverage**: You know who owes whom and can leverage minor financial pressure.

#### 20. The Keepers
Maintain the machinery, stairs, portals, and structural systems that keep the Well stable and in motion. Rarely celebrated, and often unseen.

- **Typical Roles**: Stair Mechanic, Portal Calibrator, Structural Inspector, Shaft Maintenance Crew
- **Ability Scores**: Str, Con, Cha
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Investigation, Athletics
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Tinker's Tools or Mason's Tools
- **Feat**: Savage Attacker or Tavern Brawler
- **Starting Equipment**: Mace, Tinker's Tools or Mason's Tools, Traveler's Clothes, 10 GP
- **Feature**: **Access to the Bones**: You know the hidden service corridors, maintenance shafts, and structural access points of the Well.

#### The Unbound
Those without a guild still find work to support themselves and their communities, they just do it outside of the light of the Great Cycle.

- **Typical Roles**: Corner Vendor, Scrap Broker, Ration Splitter, Urchin
- **Ability Scores**: Dex, Wis, Cha
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Choose Two: Deception, Insight, Perception, Sleight of Hand, Stealth, Survival, Persuasion
- **Tool Proficiencies**: Choose One: Disguise Kit, Thieves' Tools, Cook's Utensils, Tinker's Tools, Artisan's Tools
- **Feat**: Skilled or Alert
- **Starting Equipment**: Dagger, Chosen Toolkit, Traveler's Clothes, Belt Pouch, 15 GP
- **Feature**: **Shadow Familiarity**: You go unnoticed in the crowd, passing by without drawing immediate suspicion. You are well versed in finding unofficial labor, side work, and rumor mills.
