While exploring the Supreme Chancellor's mansion, the party found this book in the back of his private chapel.

The Cycle Canon is the sacred scripture of the Great Cycle. This book is a running commentary on the Canon by successive generations of priests and chaplains.

It contains essays, rebuttals, sermons, questions, and marginal notes. Some passages have accumulated dozens of competing interpretations in different inks. A few sections have been deliberately scraped away or struck through.

It seems to have started as a practical guide for clergy, but over time has become an anthology of theological debate, some touching on heretical subjects.

### Contents:
- The Cycle Canon: The primary text of the canon
- Glosses: Marginalia in the canon text
- Commentaries: Long essays by priests
- Disputations: Arguments between theologians
- Homilies: Sermons on the Great Cycle
- Appendices: Liturgical calendars, prayers, rites
