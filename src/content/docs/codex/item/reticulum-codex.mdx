description: Found in the Supreme Chancellor's library, an inexplicable work of the arcane.
---

Aezodus found this book while poking around in the library of the [Supreme Chancellor](/arkhive/codex/npc/chancellor/)'s mansion

At first the book looked to be filled with nonsense figures and unknown languages, but after careful study Aezodus has been able to unfold some of its secrets.

Some of those secrets include magical spells which he has been able to make use of. But it also contains writings on other arcane subjects and discoveries about the universe.

### Translations:
- The word "reticulum" was not only the first word that Aezodus was able to recognize, but it appears dozens of times throughout the volume, seemingly undefined.
- Another section seems to be talking a lot about "chambers" as in opposition to "worlds". It says "The experiment failed because the chamber was already compromised."
