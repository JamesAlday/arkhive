After dealing with the portal full of wererat farmers, Ozwald Firemountain reached out to Lilac to tell her about another portal.

This portal was left in his custody in the level 30 Custodian's repair shop, left in cold storage without any local records for it.

After the downfall of the Council when records started becoming more public, Ozwald discovered this door had records going back hundreds of years, not stating much more than that it contained a caravan.

The party looked into the caravan and confirmed that they were still traveling, and believed it might be some kind of a loop they're stuck in.
