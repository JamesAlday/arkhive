Seran was told that a VIP might be hiding within a portal that was transported to the Custodian's repair shop where Ozwald Firemountain works.

The party explored the location beyond, finding an orchard, a small farming village, and a "spa".

In the basement of the spa was a set of rooms meant as a secret hideaway for the Supreme Chancellor, but it had a squatter - Renfield Thunderbright, former Ground Councilor for The Herald.
