A grand estate that served as the residents of the Supreme Chancellor. 

The mansion is a symbol of the Chancellor's power and influence, and it is known for its opulent architecture and lavish interiors. 

The mansion was the site of a daring heist by the party, who infiltrated the estate to steal valuable artifacts and information.
