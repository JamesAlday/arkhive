- The party found the town of Karpad
- They came upon a public hanging of three fetchlings in the town square
- They convinced the Watch Captain, Lucian Groy, not to hang the prisoners
- They agreed to look for the Captain's missing daughter
- The party is also investigating the strange disease, tallowthroat, that has afflicted citizens
- They followed the Captain back to his home to look at the yard where his daughter went missing
- They found bits of candle wax in the yard
- After questioning the fetchling prisoners, the party went to investigate the chandlery
- They fought a wax golem in the back of the chandlery, and found the chandler's corpse in the basement
- They were attacked by shadows in the basement over the corpse of the chandler
- On the corpse they found a ring, 2 potions (both unidentified), and a key to the lockbox in the bedroom
- In the next room they found a tiny floating creature commanding two humans working on several huge vats of wax
