The party found the "Spa" inside the mysterious door and found Seran inside interrogating the front desk clerk.

There were guards in an upstairs bedroom and Seran convinced the guard to give him a key to the locked door.

Seran and McCoy tried talking to the guards, but they were not happy with unannounced visitors, and attacked.

Two went down easily, and two were left alive for interrogation.  Seran used his mind tricks on one of the guards after Brobor healed her back to consciousness.

She revealed that the bedroom next door was actually empty, and the VIP she was hiding was somewhere by the pool in the basement.

After finding some minor magic items in the bedroom, the party locked up the guards to go check the pool.

Seran found a secret door that led to a back hallway behind the pool, ending in a strange pool full of green slime.

Aezodus used his magic detection ritual to find that the pool was actually an illusion that hid a door.

The door led to a hidden set of rooms meant to be a secret hideout for the Supreme Chancellor.

Inside the "royal rooms" they found Renfield Thunderbright, former Grand Councilor for The Herald.

The party debated on what to do with Renfield, vacillating between killing him or bringing him to justice.
