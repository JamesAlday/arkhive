Feeling better, Bly decided to catch up with the party and found them still in the basement of the mirror mansion.

The party explored the mansion and found several rooms containing walls that appeared to be leaking a black ichor.

They found a room full of portraits, mostly of fetchlings, but also of two humans that resembled the Baron and his ancestors.

In a room where a dark fire was burning in a fireplace, they were attacked by two Ghostfire Elementals.

On the second floor, they discovered a bedroom with the corpse of one of the baron's relatives, Iozif Boroi.

They found the living quarters of a bunch of fetchlings who were trapped within the mirror.

They fetchlings introduced the party to Nicasor, a shae who had made a deal with the Baron to help him escape the mirror.

Nicasor explained that the Baron had reneged on his deal, and that he was now trapped in the mirror.

Nicasor gave the party safe passage, allowing them to rest in the sleeping quarters of the fetchlings.

Nicasor told the party that the Heart of the mirror exists in the space where the central fountain stood in the actual mansion, but the Heart had sealed off access to it.
