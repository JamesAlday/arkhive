The party made their way to the Baron's mansion
Brobor stayed back at the inn due to a fever and sore throat
They were greeted by the butler, Laurentiu, as they forced their way inside
He took them to speak with Baroness Anya
Anya had the butler take them to the Baron's study
After some coercion the Baron told them of the midnight mirror and his deal with Nicasor
The party entered the mirror and found themselves in a shadowy, mirrored version of the mansion basement
A doorway in the basement led to a crypt, guarded by two large, snakelike skeletons with venomous fangs
Seran was paralyzed by a bite from one of the creatures, but they were soon dispatched.
The party found various belongings amongst the dead interred in the crypt
