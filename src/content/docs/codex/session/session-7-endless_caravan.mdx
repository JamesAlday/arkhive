Lilac was approached by Ozwald Firemountain regarding a strange door that had been in storage with the Custodians. The door's records show that it has been visited on a number of occasions over hundreds of years and always leads to a caravan.

The party inspected the caravan, met several of the caravan's inhabitants and learned several strange facts about it.

They found a pre-existing campsite and the party took to exploring the woods while the caravan set up camp.

The party found a shrine in the woods with lots of little trinkets spread around it in offering, when Zelina came to warn them again about staying away from the deep woods.

Seran read Zelina's mind and saw a dark memory from her childhood of a savage person attacking someone from the caravan, tearing away at their throat.

The party decided to return to the Well.
