In the Slum Maze

The party explores the area and bumps into 3 more thugs.

They see the hound run past and bump into more thugs and a tough

An orc thug, Orvex, turned out to be wearing tin instead of plate when Lilac blasted him

The hound ran away and disturbed more thugs behind a locked door with Bonny giving chase

Bonny busted in the door and found thugs guarding a gang hideout

It was Thatcher Cobb and his men, thinking that he was under attack

By the time he realized it and backed off he was half dead and his gang maimed.

The party gave chase after the hound again.

It led them to a trash heap in back alleys of the lowest Lowers.

A broken pipe led them down into some kind of maintenance tunnels, still on the hunt for the hound.
