- The party decided to kill Renfield Thunderbright.
- On returning, they were asked to meet with the new Council of Guilds	
- The council asked them to look into a doorway that opened into a vast new world.	
- A previous expedition was sent into the portal, but never returned.	
- The leader of the expedition was a human woman named Artis Foxe.
- They are to see to the suitability of the land beyond the portal, and to look for the missing expedition.
- The Council gave the party a magic compass that would show their way back to the portal
- The portal opened into an ancient gateway in a hidden valley in the woods.	
- Not far from the portal they found a road, upon which traveled a cart	
- The cart's driver was a tengu merchant with a cart full of magic items and wares	
- He told them the nearest town of Karpad was not a welcoming place	
- But then again, the whole control of Nidal was not to his liking.
