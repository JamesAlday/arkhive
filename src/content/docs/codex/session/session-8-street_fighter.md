After exiting the Endless Caravan portal, the party was approached by a group of thugs working for Mr. Doyle. He requested that Bonny and the rest of the party follow them to meet with him.

The meeting took place at a warehouse in the upper Lowers, where there was a party going on. Mr. Doyle told them what the party was for - them, and the fight they were about to have with Thatcher Cobb.

The party won the fight handily, exciting the crowd into a rabid frenzy and earning the appreciation of Mr. Doyle - as well as some gold and magic items.

Later, runners delivered a message from Seran that they should return to Ozwald Firemountain, the Custodian. Upon returning, Ozwald told them that Seran had requested a particular door and went through it.

The party followed and found themselves in an orchard. They followed a dirt path that led out of the orchard and found a small farming village.

Villagers told them that Seran was either at the salon taking place in the ballroom or the spa. The party took the path to the spa and started looking for Seran.
