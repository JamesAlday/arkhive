The party catches up with the Shadow Hound around a flooded basin

There is a maintenance hatch in the flooded basin that has a blinking light

While moving towards the hound, something in the water attacks

A creature made of living water attacks but is finally subdued

The central basin is flooded and its outlets are clogged

Seran doubles back and finds the party is being hunted - they can't escape by going back

The party takes a short rest and then explores more of the tunnels

The encounter a creature made of muck and sludge

The party finds a valve off a flooded side channel that relieves the flow on the main basin

The party finds a second valve behind the sludge monster

Down another side channel they find a broken vessel or canister built into a backpack

McCoy moves towards the third valve but is attacked by some kind of green watery creature
