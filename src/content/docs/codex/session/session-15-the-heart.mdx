After resting in the midnight mansion, the party explored the main floor again.

In the north they found a room of broken furniture with a nest made by a large animal.

In the ruins they found magic items that had been chewed by the creature.

To the west they found another room with broken furniture.

This furniture, however, attacked! The party found the silver raven in the ruins.

They also found a room full of keys, among which was a chime of opening.

The east they found a small study with two fetchlings who attacked on sight.

The fetchlings were part of some revolt against the Baroi family long ago, imprisoned here for centuries.

McCoy used the glass shard to cut into the ichor walls at the center of the floor, finding the fountain courtyard.

The Heart was a giant black plant had taken over the courtyard, it's vines like veins running into the manor.

The whole place shook with tremors and felt like its collapse was imminent.

The party soon dispatched the Heart and fled back to the dining room.
