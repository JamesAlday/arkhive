The fey creature in the Chandler's basement was sacrificing villagers on a ritual altar. 
The party fought the fey, but it escaped and summoned a giant ape to fight the party.
The party defeated the ape, destroyed the altar, and freed the villagers trapped in wax.
One of the captives was Olya, the Watch Captain's daughter, who had been missing for a few days.
Inside the storage rooms in the basement, amongst the chandler's supplies, the party found the bodies of previous victims.
They collected what valuables they could from the bodies, and identified some of them as the missing expedition members from the Well.
Worn out by their battle, the party returned to the tavern to rest for the night, and to report their findings to the Watch Captain.
The planned to confront the Baron about what was happening in town the next day.
