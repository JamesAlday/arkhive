While attempting to flee the midnight mansion, the party discovered the corpse of Henric Baroi

They turned over his bedroom and found several other artifacts worth taking

The party told Nicasor and others to flee and headed towards the basement

The mansion's rooms were going strange as things started to collapse - quicksand floors, lack of gravity, complete darkness

On the way to the portal the party was attacked by two shadow hounds - one fled through the portal during the fight

Back in The Well, the party met with the Council to tell them about what happened, and that the portal was not "safe"

McCoy met with Doyle, expecting praise, but was instead given a cold warning not to meddle in places he wasn't welcome

Ozwald Firemountain delivered a nervous message to Seran telling him that the Hound was spotted in the Lowers, but he was warned not to interfere

The party decided to interfere anyway, and made for the lowers under the cover that they were going to visit Aezodus's family
