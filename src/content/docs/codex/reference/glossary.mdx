---
title: Glossary
description: A glossary of terms used in The Well
---

### Arcana
The guilds and governing organizations responsible for maintaining the Well. The [Major Aracana](#major-arcana) oversee essential functions of society, while the [Minor Arcana](#minor-arcana) support specialized trades and services.

### Chancellor, Supreme
The highest political and religious authority in the [Well](#well-the) (prior to collapse).

### Day of Reset
An important annual holiday celebrating renewal and the beginning of the new [Cycle](#great-cycle). (Post collapse it was learned that this is not a natural event, and its origin is unknown.)

### Great Cycle
The dominant religion of the [Well](#well-the), teaching that all things move through an eternal cycle of order, decay, and renewal.

See: [Great Cycle](../../lore/great-cycle) for more information.

### Guild
An organized profession or order that provides services to the [Well](#well-the). Most citizens belong to a guild.

See: [Major Arcana](../group/major-arcana) and [Minor Arcana](../group/minor-arcana) for a list of guilds.

### Household Gods
Small household shrines and protective spirits honored by many families alongside the [Great Cycle](#great-cycle).

See: [Household Gods](../../lore/household-gods) for more information.

### Major Arcana
The primary [guilds](#guild) or organizations responsible for maintaining the Well and its functions. 

See: [Major Arcana](../group/major-arcana) for a list of the Major Arcana guilds.

### Minor Arcana
The secondary [guilds](#guild) or organizations that support specialized trades and services within the Well. 

See: [Minor Arcana](../group/minor-arcana) for a list of the Minor Arcana guilds.

### Mutation
An unusual physical or magical trait possessed by some inhabitants of the [Well](#well-the). 

See: [Mutations](../../rule/mutations) for a list of known mutations.

### Portal
A gateway or passage that allows travel between different locations or demiplanes.

### Rings
The primary unit of currency used throughout the [Well](#well-the). The are made in denominations of copper, silver, and gold.

See: [Well Life: The Rings](../../lore/well-life#the-rings)

### Well, The
The vast subterranean structure that is home to the people of the campaign.

### Xod
The central divine figure worshipped by followers of the [Great Cycle](#great-cycle).

See: [Great Cycle](../../lore/great-cycle) for more information.
