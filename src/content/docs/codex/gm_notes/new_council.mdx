---
title: New Council
description: The new council that replaced the Grand Council in The Well after the fall.
---
The new council is comprised of a number of representatives who speak on behalf of groups of guilds.

- Food and Sustainability - Ruairi Butl
	- The Provider - food and resource distribution
	- The Tenders - Farmers
	- The Purifier - recycling, purification
- Health & Welfare - Owen Archer
	- The Menders - medical/bio-care
	- The Ascendant - clergy
- Infrastructure & Engineering - Catherine LeVendeur
	- The Custodian - portal maintenance
	- The Axis - engineers, vertical stability
	- The Keepers - maintenance crews
	- The Shapers - construction, habitat mgmt
	- The Kindlers - energy, fuel, lighting
	- The Luminary - Light and power
	- The Breathers - air, climate, pressure
- Civil Services - Hele d'Vitalis
	- The Concordant - coordinators/mediators
	- The Herald - comms, records
	- The Shepherd - Licensing and approvals
	- The Scriveners - Educators
	- The Reckoners - Accountants, statisticians
- Public Safety - Kendrick Ironfire
	- The Arbiter - the law
- Logistics - Ichiro San
	- The Runners - Transport, messaging, logistics
- Industry - Redeema Aldebourne
	- The Forgers - Manufacturing

Not represented:
- The Veiled Hand - chancellor security
- The Unbound
